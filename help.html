Wildlife safety guidance · Professional help

Injured, trapped or in danger?

Keep your distance. Keep children and pets away. Do not feed or try to handle the animal.

If it appears injured or trapped, contact RSPCA Queensland for advice.

1300 ANIMAL · 1300 264 625

Share the exact location privately with the rescue service, not on the public display.

A quiet encounter

Watching is often enough.

If wildlife is moving freely and shows no obvious distress, give it space and let it move on. If unsure, call for advice.
